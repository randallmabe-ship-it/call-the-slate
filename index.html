KEEP IT SIMPLE

Rules

The Weekly

Pick each NFL game straight up. Most correct wins the weekly prize pool. Monday night total score is the tiebreaker. If players are still tied after the MNF tiebreaker, the weekly prize pool is split evenly among the tied winners.

Beat The Spread

Pick the team that covers the official Call The Slate line. Once the pool line is posted, it stays fixed for scoring. Pushes are no win/no loss. Most ATS wins takes the weekly pool; MNF total score breaks ties, and any true tie remaining after the tiebreaker splits the prize pool evenly among the tied winners.

Season Championship

Season Championship scoring begins with Week 2 of the 2026 regular season. Week 1 does not count toward season standings. From Week 2 forward, Season players accumulate correct straight-up picks, and Weekly straight-up picks count automatically when enrolled in both. The season champion is the player with the most correct straight-up picks at the end of the regular season. Any final season tie is broken by total correct MNF tiebreakers across the season; if still tied, the season prize pool is split evenly among the tied champions.

Game Locks

Each game locks individually at kickoff. Later games remain editable until their own kickoff time.

Missed Picks

A game with no submitted pick before kickoff counts as no selection and cannot earn a point. Locked games cannot be changed after kickoff.

Official Results

Call The Slate scores contests from final official NFL game results. If a game is postponed or canceled, the administrator may remove it from that week's scoring when necessary.

Payouts & True Ties

Prize pools are based on verified paid entries. When the published tiebreaker cannot separate tied winners, the applicable prize pool is divided evenly among those tied winners.

Fair Play

Each entrant is responsible for submitting picks on time and using one account. Administrative corrections are limited to documented system or scoring errors and are recorded in the audit log.
