How the crown is earned

01 / Pick the winner

One point per correct straight-up pick. A tied NFL game earns no point and is recorded as a tie for all entries. No spread contest is active in this exhibition.

02 / Freeze the receipts

All house picks and tiebreakers were generated together before the opening kickoff. They cannot be edited on this site. The downloadable file and repository history preserve the original choices.

03 / Monday decides

Most correct picks wins. Closest to the combined Carolina–Tampa Bay final score breaks ties. Equal distance means shared champions. Crown only after all included games are final; postponements may delay the crown.
